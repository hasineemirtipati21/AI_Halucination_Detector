# Problem Statement
Generative AI models are widely used for research, learning, and decision-making. However, these models often generate factually incorrect information with high confidence. A critical extension of this problem is the creation of fake citations, non-existent references, and broken links that appear legitimate but cannot be verified. This leads to misinformation, legal risks, and ethical concerns, making it difficult for users to trust AI-generated content.

## Project Overview
The **AI Hallucination and Citation Verification System** is designed to help users detect unreliable AI-generated information. The system analyzes AI-generated text to identify factual claims and citations, verifies their authenticity, and flags hallucinated or unverifiable content.  

This project aims to improve trust and transparency in AI systems by clearly distinguishing between reliable and unreliable AI-produced information.

## Usage Instructions

1. Open the homepage of the application.  
2. Click the **Get Started** button to access the verification section.  
3. Paste AI-generated content into the input text area.  
4. Click the **Verify** button.  
5. Review the results:
   - Detected claims marked as **Verified**, **Uncertain**, or **Hallucinated**  
   - Citations labeled as **Valid Source**, **Broken Link**, or **Fake Reference** 
   - A reliability score indicating overall content trustworthiness
